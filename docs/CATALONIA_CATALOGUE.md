# Catalonia: audited starter subject catalogue (2026–2027)

**Scope:** This is an incomplete initial catalogue of *subject names*, not full lesson syllabi, exact centre schedules, officially published assessment objectives, or complete qualifications. A subject being listed is **not proof that it is offered in the selected school and year**. Names are deliberately kept in their official Catalan form.

## Official sources and jurisdiction

- Jurisdiction: Spain / Catalonia.
- Academic reference: 2026–2027, checked 2026-11-04.
- ESO: [Decret 175/2022 and Decret 480/2024](https://projectes.xtec.cat/nou-curriculum/educacio-basica/decret-educacio-basica/); [Decret 480/2024, Annex 7](https://projectes.xtec.cat/nou-curriculum/wp-content/uploads/usu2072/2024/12/DECRET-4802024-de-17-de-desembre-de-modificacio-del-Decret-1752022-de-27-de-setembre-dordenacio-dels-ensenyaments-de-leducacio-basica.pdf). The 2024 amendment's new schedule was implemented in 2025–2026.
- Batxillerat: [Decret 171/2022 and Decret 103/2026](https://projectes.xtec.cat/nou-curriculum/batxillerat/decret-batxillerat/); [final approval record for Decret 103/2026](https://governobert.gencat.cat/ca/transparencia/normativa-i-informacio-interes-juridic/normativa/normativa-en-tramit/edu/en-tramit/dbatxilleratmod). The 2026 amendment applies to 1st-year content in 2026–2027; remaining 2nd-year changes are scheduled in 2027–2028.

## What is represented

- ESO years 1–3: six subjects explicitly present in **all three years** under Annex 7, plus science/arts/music/technology subjects whose placement depends on the centre. The small optional sample is clearly flagged.
- ESO year 4: seven fixed subjects and one verified optional subject, Cultura Científica; the many other electives are not yet complete.
- Batxillerat year 1: common subjects and selected modality subjects (science, general and social-science mathematics, languages, technology and other verified examples), not the entire official offering.
- Batxillerat year 2: common subjects and selected modality subjects. Check applicable transition rules for 2027–2028.

The original published curricula have granular learning outcomes, competencies, evaluation criteria and knowledge requirements. These have **not been copied** into the starter. No private textbooks or derivative content have been uploaded to GitHub.

## Data integrity rules for expansion

1. Keep separate country, jurisdiction, education stage, subject, programme, institution and academic-year IDs. Never make translated display strings the data keys.
2. For each new subject or module, store a primary source URL, original name, source date/version, effective years, availability category (common, optional, modality, school-dependent), and reviewer.
3. For first-to-third ESO, **do not present science, visual arts, music or technology as mandatory in each individual year**, because distribution across years varies by centre.
4. For Batxillerat, keep **modalities** separate; a modality subject must not be represented as a common subject for all learners.
5. Only add officially verified subject lists. Changes to decrees must be checked annually, including the 2027–2028 second-year transition.
6. University modules and vocational qualifications will require different schemas and institutional/qualification identifiers.
7. Media files (audio, images and videos) require Blas Llavero's approval prior to any repository upload.

## Status

This is a useful **offline subset for navigation testing**. Lesson generation, avatar speaking, curricular mappings by topics, tests and PDF exports remain unimplemented.
