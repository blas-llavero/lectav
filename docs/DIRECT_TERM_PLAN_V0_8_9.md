# LECTAV 0.8.9 — Generate trimester plans directly from course selection

## Direct access

The teacher selects a course and subject on the home screen and can open **Generate trimester plan with ChatGPT** without first navigating to a separate course-plan screen. The selected course and subject are passed to the generator; if the course has not been saved, it can be stored before plan creation.

## Generation sequence

The teacher chooses trimester (1–3), academic year, total number of sessions (6–48) and a model offered by their authenticated ChatGPT account. A proposal typically contains 2–8 units with named, distinct lessons. The proposed totals must match the requested number of sessions exactly.

LECTAV shows the proposed index for teacher review before saving; no unit order is claimed to be mandated by law. The app stores each trimester independently and preserves other trimesters on update. Replacing the same trimester requires an explicit warning because it may replace previously reviewed content/progress.

## Curriculum provenance

When an official curriculum PDF is available, LECTAV can retrieve it on demand, extract relevant text locally and use an attributed excerpt as generation context. An unsupported or unverified curriculum crosswalk must be described as a proposed draft, not as official regulatory compliance.

## Test plan

Verify direct navigation from a chosen course, authorization checks, exact session counts, teacher edits, preservation of unrelated trimesters, re-entry, material generation prefilled with the correct topic, and encrypted backup/restore of all associated plans and materials.
